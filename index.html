Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Your complete guide to buying a Ready-for-Occupancy condo — from reservation to move-in.

What areas do you handle for RFO units?
I specialize in Ready-for-Occupancy condominiums across Metro Manila's premier business districts — Uptown Bonifacio (BGC), McKinley West, McKinley Hill, Newport City, Manhattan Garden City, and Makati CBD. If you're looking in any of these locations, I'll help you find the best available unit for your budget, lifestyle, and investment goals.
How much is the reservation fee?
The reservation fee typically ranges from ₱30,000 to ₱60,000, depending on the project and unit you choose. It secures your selected unit and starts the purchase process. Message me for the latest pricing, promos, and unit availability.
Can I move in right away?
Yes. Most Ready-for-Occupancy units allow move-in with only a 3% to 5% down payment, once the required documentation and financing approval are complete.
Is the unit semi-furnished or bare?
It depends on the project and unit. Most Megaworld RFO units include premium finishes such as:
  • Modular kitchen cabinets
  • Kitchen countertop and sink
  • Built-in wardrobes (selected units)
  • Complete toilet and bath fixtures
  • Split-type air-conditioning provisions or installed units (selected projects)
I'll gladly recommend the best option based on your preferences and budget.
Can foreigners buy a condominium in the Philippines?
Yes. Foreign nationals may own condominium units in the Philippines, provided foreign ownership within the project does not exceed the legal limit. I can guide you through the requirements and the entire buying process.
Can OFWs purchase a condo while abroad?
Absolutely. OFWs can reserve and purchase a property even while overseas. I assist with online reservation, virtual property presentations, digital document processing, bank financing assistance, and secure payment options — everything can be processed conveniently online.
Do you assist with bank financing?
Yes. I can guide you through the bank financing process and help prepare the necessary requirements from start to finish.
How can I schedule a property viewing?
It's easy — message me on Viber, WhatsApp, or Facebook, or use the "Schedule a Viewing" button on this page. Private, virtual, and on-site viewings are available by appointment.
